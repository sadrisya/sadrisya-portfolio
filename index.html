About Me

Building with purpose.

A brief look at who I am and what drives my work.

I'm a Front-End Developer with nearly 7 years of experience building modern, performant web applications. My core expertise lies in JavaScript, Ruby on Rails, Tailwind CSS, HTML, CSS, and Stimulus — and I'm actively expanding into React.
